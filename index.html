Block & Bloom

A calm block puzzle that grows a garden. Drag flower, leaf and water tiles onto the board, clear rows and columns, and every game grows your garden a little more.

Help and questions

Write to us at support@rioticstudio.com. It helps if you tell us your iPhone model and, for anything about your data, the random ID shown at the bottom of the game's Settings.

Common questions

Does the game save automatically?
Yes, after every move. You can close it at any time and carry on where you left off. Your progress is kept on your phone, so deleting the game also deletes your progress.
How do I turn reminders off?
In the game, open Settings from the home screen and switch off each reminder you don't want. You can also turn off all notifications for Block & Bloom in your iPhone's Settings.
Can I stop the game sending play data?
Yes. In the game's Settings, switch off Share anonymous play data. Nothing more is sent. See the privacy policy for what it sends and why.
How do I get my data deleted?
Email us the random ID from the bottom of the game's Settings and we'll delete what we hold for it.
Why do Journey levels sometimes give fewer pieces?
Journey levels adapt to how you play. If you've been winning them easily, later levels give you fewer pieces; if one is hard for you, you get more again. The number of pieces is always shown before you start, and the pieces themselves are never made harder.
Is it fair?
Yes. The board never cheats, the pieces never depend on what you've spent, and scores are exactly what you earned.
